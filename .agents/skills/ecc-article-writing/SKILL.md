# Article writing

Identify the audience, purpose, medium, and any supplied voice examples. Outline one job per section. Lead with a concrete example, artifact, or fact, then explain it. Preserve the user's voice and use evidence instead of inflated claims. Do not invent quotes, experience, results, or sources.

Revise for pacing, clear structure, and repetition. Check factual claims against provided or retrieved sources and label what remains uncertain. Deliver the finished draft in the requested format; ask for missing voice guidance only when it materially changes the result.
