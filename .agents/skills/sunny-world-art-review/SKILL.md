# Sunny world and art review

Read `docs/art-bible.md`, `docs/world-bible.md`, and `public/assets/ARTWORK.md` for the affected asset. Compare new work with the existing Chopper and Sunny anchors at its actual displayed size. Check silhouette, perspective, palette, atlas geometry, foot anchor, alpha, provenance, and reduced-motion behavior where relevant.

Inspect the rendered normal, working, waiting or blocked, and reduced-motion states that the change affects. Keep readable labels and direct keyboard controls. Report what was visually inspected, what remains unverified, and any mismatch with the asset contract. Animation and generated art do not prove task progress or mission completion.
