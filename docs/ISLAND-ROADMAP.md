# Island expansion

## Available in 0.7.0

- Desktop ship and full-app island with six districts, zoom and walking sprites.
- Ten core crewmates and four optional specialists with editable jobs, conversation and memory.
- Persistent local job definitions, sequential execution, review gates, stop controls and soft usage/time thresholds.
- Luffy delegation and model/reasoning selection within the existing account catalog.
- Draft-oriented product, digital content and client work templates; manually recorded receipts.

## Growth path

1. Pick one repeatable deliverable and review several successful runs. Measure usable output, actual usage and customer demand before increasing frequency.
2. Add specialists through the resident catalog and matching sprite rows. Separate projects provide clear work boundaries; this release still runs one island job at a time.
3. Add a durable worker queue and isolated checkouts before increasing concurrent execution. Distributed workers, remote recovery and multi-machine operation are future work.
4. Connect a chosen business channel with explicit permissions and reviewable drafts. Channel-specific publishing, customer communications, payment collection and accounting integrations are future work.
5. Expand autonomy only after reliable verification, failure recovery and real cost accounting exist for that workflow.

The island is a local orchestration foundation, not yet a distributed agent farm or an income-generating business. No business schedules or external actions are activated by installation.
