# Welcome aboard

Step into Sunny HQ, Captain. There is room at this table for a bold idea, a rough sketch, or a question you have yet to untangle. Bring your curiosity; every good voyage begins with a direction worth exploring.

You set the course. The crew helps shape the work into something you can examine, improve, and decide how to use. Start small enough to see the shore, and leave room for discovery along the way.

## Your first voyage

Choose the project you want to move forward. Give the crew a clear mission: what you need, who it serves, and which limits matter. Describe success through observable criteria, such as a draft with three sections, a working example, or a file you can open and inspect.

Name the expected result and explain how you will judge it. If the course changes, update the mission so everyone follows the same map.

## Captain review

Inspect the actual results against your criteria. Read the file, try the example, and ask for evidence where something remains uncertain. A confident report or an ended work session does not make a mission complete.

Request changes when needed. Personally authorize consequential actions before they happen, including publishing, sending messages, spending money, or deleting important material. Your review sets the next heading, and your approval gives the crew permission to sail onward.
